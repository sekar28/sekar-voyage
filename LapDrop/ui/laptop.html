The phone will warn that the connection is not private the first time. That is expected: choose Advanced or Show details, then continue to the site.

No Wi-Fi around?

Use the phone's hotspot. Turn on Personal Hotspot on the phone and connect this laptop to it. The code above updates by itself. Transfers stay between the two devices and do not use mobile data.

Or use the laptop's hotspot. Turn on Mobile hotspot in Windows, join it from the phone, then pick Laptop hotspot under Network. Windows only allows this while the laptop has some connection to share.

Send from the phone's Share menu

Open LapDrop on the phone and follow "Add LapDrop to the Share menu" there. After that, Share → LapDrop sends straight here, on Wi-Fi or hotspot.
